## Definition

The DIKW pyramid describes 4 layers of data
transformation: Data → Information → Knowledge → Wisdom.

## Explanation

- **Data**: raw, unprocessed facts and figures
  without context or meaning.
- **Information**: data that has been organized and
  structured so that it has meaning.
- **Knowledge**: information combined with
  experience and interpretation, allowing understanding and
  insight.
- **Wisdom**: the ability to apply knowledge to make
  decisions.
- This framework recurs throughout the course:
  [[data-driven-decision-making]] frames the reason for data-driven
  decision making as exactly "turning data into information → knowledge →
  wisdom."
