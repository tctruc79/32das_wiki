## Definition

Data-analytic thinking is the approach that precedes
data-driven decision making — it provides the "compass," while
data-driven decision making provides the "movement" that follows.

## Explanation

- **Data-analytic thinking comes first, data-driven
  decision making follows**: data-analytic thinking provides the
  "compass," then data-driven decision making provides the
  "movement."
